Web Design Steps

  • Determine the intended audiencee

    The colours, images, fonts and layout should be tailored to the preference of your audience.

  • Determine the Goals of the Site

    What do you want the user to do when they visit your site? Do you want them to buy something, sign up for a newsletter, or just read your content?

  • Sketch a Tentative Page Layout

    Be consistent in your overall layout and colour. Use a grid to align eleents.
